How it works

  • Program: 10 November 2026 to 31 January 2027.
  • Add the app to your Home Screen first. iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome → ⋮ → Add to Home screen. Then always open it from there.
  • Do the six exercises once a day, 1–2 minutes each, at the same time and place.
  • Open the app and tap the answers for today. It saves by itself.
  • Two phones: once both phones are connected, entries appear on both phones by themselves. See Two phones below.
  • No answer for an exercise means you did not do it that day. Tap 0 if you tried and she did not manage.

The six exercises

Keep it playful

  • Sit opposite her. Show it first, then let her copy you.
  • Hold back the next piece until she looks at you, then give it at once.
  • Say one word for each action: “gone!”, “in!”, “kick!”, “hop!”, “thank you!”.
  • Guiding her hand gently is fine. If she screams or panics, stop and try again later.
  • First one short exercise, then her favourite game.

Videos

  • Film one exercise for 30–60 seconds, 1–2 times a week, so the doctor can see the progress.
  • Over two weeks, try to film each of the six exercises once.
  • Hold the phone still and film from the side, so her hands, feet and eyes can be seen.
  • Add every video to the shared album. iPhone: Photos → open the video → Share → Add to Shared Album. Android: Google Photos → open the video → Share → Add to album.
  • Then tap the exercise under Video on that day, so both phones know what was filmed.

Safety

  • Sunflower seeds: stay right next to her the whole time. No marbles.
  • No real candle. Use a torch or an LED candle.
  • Boards flat on the floor. Never chairs or anything high.
  • Use a plastic bottle for the tray, never glass.
